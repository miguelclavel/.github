# Code of conduct

These repos are for people learning from each other, at every level of experience. Keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most questions come from someone stuck, not someone careless.
- Critique the work, not the person, and say what would make it better.
- Credit other people's work when you build on it.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about anyone's identity or background.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and self promotion that has nothing to do with the discussion.

## If something happens

Email hello@miguelclavel.com. I'll read every report, keep it confidential, and act on it: from a reminder of these rules to removing comments or blocking someone from the repos.
